# Chess of Dark — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Developer:** KeeCOr  
**Contact:** koheag2@stoicent.com

---

## 1. Overview

Chess of Dark is a single-player desktop game developed by KeeCOr. This policy describes what data the game collects, stores, and transmits.

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect

Chess of Dark does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Personal identifying information (name, email, address)
- Demographic data
- Behavioral analytics or telemetry
- Advertising identifiers
- Location data

## 3. Steam Integration

When launched through Steam, Chess of Dark uses the **Steamworks SDK** to access:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Steam display name | Show in-game username |
| Achievement status | Unlock and read Steam achievements |
| Steam Cloud save data | Sync game progress across devices |
| Steam Leaderboard entries | Submit and read high scores |

This data is handled by Valve Corporation under the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/). KeeCOr does not receive or store this data independently.

## 4. Local Data Storage

Chess of Dark saves game state locally on your device:

- **Save files** — stored in the application data directory
- **Settings** — stored locally in app preferences

This data never leaves your device unless Steam Cloud sync is enabled (opt-in through Steam settings).

## 5. No Third-Party Services

Chess of Dark does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, social media, or crash-reporting services beyond Steamworks.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Chess of Dark does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The game contains no online features, chat, or user-generated content.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the game's Steam store page and this document. Continued use of the game constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
