## Full Description

**Chess of Dark** takes the familiar rules of chess and compresses them into a fast, tense 5x5 battlefield.

Each turn asks a simple question: move a piece now, summon a new one with mana, or end the turn and force the enemy to answer your position. Pawns hold the line early, but every turn adds mana that can become a knight, bishop, rook, or queen at the right moment.

The result is a short-form strategy game about tempo, pressure, and timing. Read the threats around your king, open space with captures, save mana for a decisive summon, and checkmate the enemy before your own position collapses.

<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
  <li><b>Compact 5x5 Tactical Battles</b> — Short, replayable matches built for decisive play. No opening theory. No long endgames. Every position demands a read.</li>
  <li><b>Mana-Driven Summoning</b> — Spend accumulated mana to place pawns, knights, bishops, rooks, or queens near your king. Timing a high-cost summon decides games.</li>
  <li><b>Standard Chess Movement</b> — Pieces move and capture exactly as in chess. The rules you already know, applied in a space where every square matters more.</li>
  <li><b>Three-Phase Turn Structure</b> — Move, summon, or commit your turn without summoning. Skipping a summon is a valid tempo play that forces the enemy's hand.</li>
  <li><b>AI Difficulty Progression</b> — From a first match with minimal pressure to hard-mode opponents that punish passive positions and exploit mana timing mistakes.</li>
  <li><b>Immediate Combat Feedback</b> — Captures, checks, promotions, and checkmate all resolve with clear visual confirmation before the next decision begins.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Design Philosophy</h3>
Chess of Dark is built for players who want a strategy game they can finish in one sitting. A single match takes minutes. Mastery comes from reading tempo — knowing when to summon, when to move, and when to let your opponent run out of good options.
